# Asset provenance

- `src/render/artwork.ts`: original procedural Canvas illustrations created for Inkblast. They become local Pixi textures at startup. No original Atomic Bomberman art is copied or loaded.
- `public/hud/*.webp`: 22 original inventory illustrations generated individually with built-in OpenAI Image Gen from the selected player-panel concept, then exported as transparent 256px WebP assets. They cover all 13 pickup types and nine disease outcomes, including the instant position swap. Prompts requested matching dark ink outlines, cream rims, saturated fills and silhouettes readable at 32–52px, without text except the mystery cube's question mark. These assets are served locally and do not add a runtime service.

  Temporary-effect subjects: an amber snail (slow), orange sneaker with blue streaks (fast), crossed-out bomb (no bombs), trailing bombs (leaking bombs), small drooping flame with a blue droplet (weak flames), speed-streaked bombs (rapid leaking), almost-burned fuse with a large spark (short fuses), red/blue helmets exchanging places (swap), and opposing bent arrows (reversed controls). Additional pickup subjects: crowned gold flame, lime skull, paired purple skulls, and a cream/gold mystery cube.
- `public/share/inkblast-v1.jpg`, `public/favicon.png`, and `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: share artwork and icons composed from the same original game textures and bundled Barlow font. Regenerate with `npm run render:share` (requires Playwright Chromium), or `CHROME_CHANNEL=chrome npm run render:share` to use installed Google Chrome. These committed static assets need no browser rendering at build time or when a link is shared. When changing the share image, use a new versioned filename and update the absolute social image URLs in `index.html` to help refresh image caches.
- `public/audio/music/fuse-funk.mp3`: original instrumental arcade electro-funk battle music generated with ElevenLabs Music v2. A 64-second take was normalized to -16 LUFS, encoded at 160 kbps and edited with a 160 ms wrap crossfade into a 63.84-second loop. The brief requested punchy breakbeats, playful synth bass, chip arpeggios and brass-like synth stabs, without vocals or references to existing artists or tracks.
- `public/audio/effects/{explode,pickup,result}.mp3`: original short comic explosion, power-up sparkle and round-victory stinger generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2. Leading silence was trimmed, levels normalized to -14 LUFS with a -2 dB true-peak target and short tail fades applied before 128 kbps encoding. Full prompts, generation IDs, models and settings are in `public/audio/source.json`; alternate takes remain in the linked ElevenLabs flow. Bundled playback uses only local static files through Web Audio; no ElevenLabs account, API key or service request is needed at runtime.
- `src/audio/sound.ts`: the original device-synthesized music and effects remain as loading/error fallbacks. Character chirps, bomb placement, death, kick/throw, warp and hurry cues remain synthesized. Music starts after a player gesture, follows mute/pause/round end, and runs 12% faster during hurry time. Explosion samples are rate-limited during chain reactions.
- `public/fonts/barlow-condensed-latin-800-normal.woff2`: Barlow Condensed by Jeremy Tribby, distributed by Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. The license is bundled at `public/fonts/OFL.txt`.
- `docs/reference/inkblast-concept.png`: user-supplied visual direction. This reference is not loaded by the game and is not used as a static arena background.

Eleven historical arena names and mechanic families are documented in the reference audit. Theme artwork and the twelve bundled duel schemes are Inkblast interpretations.
